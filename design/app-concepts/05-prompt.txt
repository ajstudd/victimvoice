Use case: ui-mockup. Produce an EXCEPTIONALLY well-designed, premium Android women's safety app concept board, "Victim Voice". This is a COMPLETE REDESIGN of a bland generic white-and-pink card app. The deliverable is ONE high resolution portrait board showing SIX complete mobile UI screens, 3 columns by 2 rows, carefully aligned. Each actual screen must be generous and readable, minimal hairline device frame, NO thick shiny phone hardware, NO drop shadows around phones. Neutral matte pale backdrop, ample clear gutters. Board heading "Victim Voice — A more considered companion". Small board footer "Visual proposal · Illustrative content". The six screens need to look like a real outstanding mobile product, not a marketing landing page or template.
ART DIRECTION: Warm, composed, distinctive, beautifully balanced. Palette: midnight plum #30253D for important headings/large intentional feature surfaces and primary non-emergency buttons, warm parchment #FBF7F2 for the screen background, vivid terracotta-coral #D84D46 exclusively for SOS/urgent actions, jade #397568 for meaningful active states, pale peach #F5DECC and lilac #ECE5F0 as purposeful section surfaces. Deep dark readable type. NO low contrast blush-on-white. Refined humanist sans typography, large short confident headings, precise smaller labels, beautiful editorial line breaks. Android-native familiar components and controls with generous touch targets. Shapes vary with PURPOSE: full-width visual feature area, quiet divided text rows, contact portraits in circles, honest timeline, compact chips, large clear actions. Avoid stacking identical rounded cards. Illustration, if any, must be one finely crafted minimal editorial line artwork conveying people connected, never a cartoon woman/shield decoration. No stock photos, neon/glows/glass, gradient text, safety scores or ornamental progress rings. Meaningful visuals lead: actual contact connection diagram, actual travel route, actual time countdown, file timeline. Every element earns its place.
NAVIGATION: For regular screens use the exact consistent understated four-tab bottom bar "Home", "My Circle", "Guide", "My Space". Small outlined line icons with selected state. Emergency access is prominent and consistent in daily screens. Do not show a busy navigation bar on full-screen emergency check-in.
